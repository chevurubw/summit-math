---
title: "Vieta's formulas"
---

Here's a surprising fact: you can know the **sum** and **product** of a polynomial's roots without ever finding the roots. The coefficients tell you directly. These relationships are **Vieta's formulas**, and they let you answer questions like "what is $r^2 + s^2$?" in two lines, even when $r$ and $s$ are ugly.

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

- read the sum and product of roots straight off a quadratic,
- do the same for cubics,
- compute expressions like $r^2 + s^2$, $\tfrac{1}{r} + \tfrac{1}{s}$ and $r^3 + s^3$ from the coefficients,
- build a polynomial from facts about its roots.

## Why it works

If a quadratic has roots $r$ and $s$, it factors:

$$
x^2 + bx + c = (x - r)(x - s) = x^2 - (r + s)x + rs.
$$

Match the coefficients: $r + s = -b$ and $rs = c$. That's the whole idea.

<Formula>

For $ax^2 + bx + c$ with roots $r, s$:

$$
r + s = -\frac{b}{a}, \qquad rs = \frac{c}{a}.
$$

</Formula>

Drag the sliders to choose a sum and a product. The parabola $y = x^2 - Sx + P$ always has roots with exactly that sum and product (when it crosses the axis at all).

## Expressions in the roots

Most Vieta problems ask for something symmetric in the roots. Rewrite it using only $r + s$ and $rs$.

<Formula>

$$
\begin{aligned}
r^2 + s^2 &= (r + s)^2 - 2rs\\
\frac{1}{r} + \frac{1}{s} &= \frac{r + s}{rs}\\
(r - s)^2 &= (r + s)^2 - 4rs\\
r^3 + s^3 &= (r + s)^3 - 3rs(r + s)
\end{aligned}
$$

</Formula>

<Steps problem="The roots of $2x^2 - 6x + 5 = 0$ are $r$ and $s$. Find $r^2 + s^2$.">
<Step title="Vieta">
$r + s = \tfrac{6}{2} = 3$ and $rs = \tfrac{5}{2}$.
</Step>
<Step title="Rewrite">
$r^2 + s^2 = 3^2 - 2 \cdot \tfrac{5}{2} = 9 - 5 = 4$.
</Step>
</Steps>

Notice the roots here are complex (the discriminant is $36 - 40 < 0$), yet the answer is a nice real number. Vieta never cares.

<Check choices={["$13$", "$16$", "$19$", "$22$", "$25$"]} answer={2} explain="$r + s = 5$ and $rs = 3$, so $r^2 + s^2 = 25 - 6 = 19$.">
If $r$ and $s$ are the roots of $x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0$, what is $r^2 + s^2$?
</Check>

<Check choices={["$9$", "$12$", "$15$", "$18$", "$20$"]} answer={3} explain="Call the roots $r$ and $2r$. Their sum is $3r = 9$, so $r = 3$ and the roots are 3 and 6. Then $k = 3 \cdot 6 = 18$.">
One root of $x^2 - 9x + k = 0$ is twice the other. What is $k$?
</Check>

## Cubics

For $x^3 + bx^2 + cx + d$ with roots $r, s, t$, expanding $(x - r)(x - s)(x - t)$ gives

<Formula>

$$
r + s + t = -b, \qquad rs + st + tr = c, \qquad rst = -d.
$$

With a leading coefficient $a$, divide each by $a$. The signs alternate: minus, plus, minus.

</Formula>

<Steps problem="The roots of $x^3 - 6x^2 + 11x - 6$ are $r, s, t$. Find $\tfrac{1}{r} + \tfrac{1}{s} + \tfrac{1}{t}$.">
<Step title="Common denominator">
$\dfrac{1}{r} + \dfrac{1}{s} + \dfrac{1}{t} = \dfrac{st + rt + rs}{rst}$.
</Step>
<Step title="Vieta">
$rs + st + tr = 11$ and $rst = 6$, so the answer is $\tfrac{11}{6}$.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Check choices={["$4$", "$10$", "$14$", "$16$", "$22$"]} answer={2} explain="$r + s + t = 4$ and $rs + st + tr = 1$. Then $r^2 + s^2 + t^2 = (r + s + t)^2 - 2(rs + st + tr) = 16 - 2 = 14$.">
The roots of $x^3 - 4x^2 + x + 7 = 0$ are $r, s, t$. What is $r^2 + s^2 + t^2$?
</Check>

<Trap>
Watch the signs. The sum of the roots is the **negative** of the second coefficient (divided by the leading one). For a cubic the product is $-d/a$, but for a quartic it is $+e/a$.
</Trap>

<Tip>
Going backwards is just as useful: the monic quadratic with roots $r$ and $s$ is $x^2 - (r + s)x + rs$. To find the quadratic whose roots are $r^2$ and $s^2$, compute $r^2 + s^2$ and $r^2s^2$.
</Tip>

<Recap>
- Quadratic $ax^2 + bx + c$: sum $-b/a$, product $c/a$.
- Cubic: sum $-b/a$, pair sum $c/a$, product $-d/a$.
- Rewrite symmetric expressions using the sum and product.
- Vieta works even when the roots are complex.
</Recap>
